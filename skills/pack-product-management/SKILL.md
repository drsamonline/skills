# Product management pack

77 skills, each kept as its own skill in `skills/<id>/` — this pack is the index that tells you which one to read. Drawn from 1 upstream repositories.

## When to use

- Framing a problem or validating an opportunity before building.
- Writing a PRD, spec or one-pager.
- Prioritising a backlog or shaping a roadmap.
- Communicating a decision to stakeholders or running a launch review.
