# Clinical and homeopathy books — safety framing and voice

**Scope: medical, clinical and homeopathy books only.** For any other kind of
book — memoir, general non-fiction, poetry, business, self-help — ignore this
file entirely; `content-rules.md` and the QA gate are the whole of it. Nothing
here belongs in a non-medical book.

These rules come from the `drbooks` project notes and **add to**
`content-rules.md`.

## Voice (clinical books)

- Plain, confident, unhurried sentences — the same banned list as
  `content-rules.md` §Language applies.
- **Present classical or historical material as such** — "classical literature
  describes…", "traditionally associated with…", "<author> proposed…" — never as
  settled modern fact. Accurate attribution is also what keeps the content honest
  (see Safety framing).
- **Label each chapter for its reader.** A small coloured badge at the top of a
  section names the audience it serves, so a patient-facing passage and a
  practitioner-facing one are never confused. Choose the set that fits the book;
  a homeopathy text written for mixed readers, for example, might use *For
  Families & Seekers* (plain, patient-facing), *For the Medical Student*
  (mechanism and reasoning), *For the Collegiate Preceptor* (teaching cases and
  diagrams) and *For the Bedside Healer* (posology and practical dosing). Pick
  whichever fits each chapter; do not force every audience into every chapter.
- **Illustrative clinical vignettes are labelled composites** — e.g.
  "Illustrative Teaching Case", never a verified patient record, and never with
  fabricated precise outcome statistics.

## Safety framing (non-negotiable)

Rewrite, rather than include "for historical completeness", any source passage
that could lead a reader to delay real care:

- **Any acute emergency** (postpartum haemorrhage, status epilepticus/seizures,
  severe pneumonia, cholera/dysentery with dehydration, febrile convulsions) gets
  an explicit boxed note: the real medical threshold (e.g. "a seizure lasting
  more than five minutes"), and a plain statement that the remedy is an adjunct
  alongside conventional emergency care, never a substitute.
- **Suicidal ideation or despair** as a classical keynote gets a crisis-line box.
  **Verify the number with a live search before using it** — helplines merge and
  change (India's Kiran helpline was absorbed into Tele-MANAS at 14416 /
  1-800-891-4416). Never reuse a memorised number.
- **Causal claims for serious conditions without evidence** (e.g. "maternal
  stress causes autism") are reframed as "a historical hypothesis, not an
  established cause", with a line that standard evaluation must never be delayed.
- **Vaccine-timing claims** are reframed as classical observation about symptom
  timing, explicitly not a vaccine-safety claim, pointing back to a conventional
  physician for vaccination decisions.
- **Unverifiable specifics** (a named-but-unconfirmed hospital, a precise
  epidemic-year statistic, an unattributed quote) are dropped or generalised —
  do not launder an unverifiable factoid into new phrasing.
- **Author-bio and institutional facts:** only ever state what the person has told
  you. Never invent hospital names, credentials or numbers.
- State plainly, where relevant: homeopathy never replaces emergency
  resuscitation, mechanical ventilation, IV fluids, surgical consults or
  indicated antibiotics; the evidence base is contested; there are no
  homeopathic vaccines; dosing advice is general and subject to a licensed
  clinician.

The general rules this file relies on — no invented facts, composite labelling,
honest limits, the banned word list — are in `content-rules.md`; the extra
verification checks that apply to *any* book are in `qa-checklist.md`.
