# Channel formats

Shape, length, and rules for each channel. Adapt when the brief specifies otherwise.

## Blog post

- **Headline:** specific and benefit- or curiosity-led, under ~65 characters. Offer 2-3 options.
- **Intro:** 2-3 sentences — hook, stakes, and the promise of what the reader gets. No throat-clearing.
- **Body:** 3-5 scannable sections with subheads, one idea each, concrete examples over abstractions.
- **Close:** short recap plus one clear call to action.
- **Length:** ~700-1200 words unless the brief says otherwise.

## Social copy

**LinkedIn**
- Hook in the first 2 lines — that's all that shows before "see more". It must earn the expand.
- Body: 3-6 short paragraphs or a tight list, one idea, line breaks for scannability.
- Close with a question or a CTA. 3-5 relevant hashtags. ~150-300 words.

**X / Twitter**
- Single post: one idea, under ~280 characters, no wasted words.
- Thread: hook first, then numbered posts, each self-contained and worth the next tap.

**Instagram**
- Caption opens with a hook, then 2-4 short lines, sparing emoji, a CTA, and 5-10 hashtags.

## Newsletter

- **Subject line:** under ~50 characters, specific; give 2-3 options.
- **Preview / preheader text:** ~40-90 characters that complements the subject rather than repeating it.
- **Opener:** personal and one-to-one — first person, as if writing to a single reader.
- **Body:** 2-4 scannable sections, links where useful, one primary CTA.
- **Sign-off:** a short human note. Flag any personalization tokens (e.g. `{{first_name}}`).

## Adapting

A single brief often spans several channels. Write the anchor piece first (usually the blog post or newsletter), then derive the social cuts from it — don't write the channels in isolation.
