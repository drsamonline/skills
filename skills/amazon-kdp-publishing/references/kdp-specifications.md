# KDP print specifications

Verified against KDP's own help pages. Where a figure is a minimum KDP
enforces, it says so; where it is a recommendation, it says that instead.

Sources: *Print Options* (G201834180), *Set Trim Size, Bleed, and Margins*
(GVBQ3CMEQW3W2VL6), *Paperback Submission Guidelines* (G201857950), *Create a

## 1. Binding types

| | Paperback | Hardcover (case laminate) |
|---|---|---|
| Trim sizes | 16 standard + custom | 5 fixed |
| Page range | 24–828 (depends on trim/paper) | 75–550 |
| Cover file | one spread, back+spine+front | one spread with wrap and hinges |
| Dust jacket | no | no — the case *is* the cover |
| Spine text | needs 79+ pages | available |
| Standard colour | yes | **no** |

Custom paperback trim: width 4–8.5 in, height 6–11.69 in. A trim is "large"
(and costs more to print) if width > 6.12 in or height > 9 in.

## 2. Ink and paper

| Option | Paperback | Hardcover |
|---|---|---|
| Black ink, cream paper (50–61 lb / 74–90 GSM) | yes | yes |
| Black ink, white paper | yes | yes |
| Black ink, groundwood (45 lb / 60 GSM) | yes | **no** |
| Standard colour, white | yes | **no** |
| Premium colour, white (60–71 lb / 88–105 GSM) | yes | yes |

Ink type is **locked after publication** (ISBN requirement). You cannot move a
published book between standard colour, premium colour and black-and-white. If
you need both, set up two separate books.

Black-and-white books can switch cream ↔ white ↔ groundwood after publishing,
but you must re-check the cover, because the spine width changes.

## 3. Trim sizes and page limits

Page limits are **minimum–maximum** and depend on ink, paper and trim together.
This is the table that decides whether a book is publishable at a given size.

### Paperback

| Trim (in) | White | Cream | Groundwood | Std colour | Premium |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 × 8 | 24–828 | 24–776 | 24–812 | 72–600 | 24–828 |
| 5.06 × 7.81 | 24–828 | 24–776 | 24–812 | 72–600 | 24–828 |
| 5.25 × 8 | 24–828 | 24–776 | 24–812 | 72–600 | 24–828 |
| 5.5 × 8.5 | 24–828 | 24–776 | 24–812 | 72–600 | 24–828 |
| 6 × 9 | 24–828 | 24–776 | 24–812 | 72–600 | 24–828 |
| 6.14 × 9.21 | 24–828 | 24–776 | 24–812 | 72–600 | 24–828 |
| 6.69 × 9.61 | 24–828 | 24–776 | 24–812 | 72–600 | 24–828 |
| 7 × 10 | 24–828 | 24–776 | 24–812 | 72–600 | 24–828 |
| 7.44 × 9.69 | 24–828 | 24–776 | 24–812 | 72–600 | 24–828 |
| 7.5 × 9.25 | 24–828 | 24–776 | 24–812 | 72–600 | 24–828 |
| 8 × 10 | 24–828 | 24–776 | 24–812 | 72–600 | 24–828 |
| 8.25 × 6 | 24–800 | 24–750 | 24–784 | 72–600 | 24–800 |
| 8.25 × 8.25 | 24–800 | 24–750 | 24–784 | 72–600 | 24–800 |
| A4 (8.27 × 11.69) | 24–780 | 24–730 | 24–764 | — | 24–590 |

**The counter-intuitive row:** 8.5 × 11 caps at 590 pages while 6 × 9 carries
828. A long manuscript rejected at 8.5 × 11 usually fits at 6 × 9. Moving to a
*larger* trim makes a page-count problem worse.

### Hardcover

Five trims only, all **75–550 pages** on every available paper:

`5.5 × 8.5` · `6 × 9` · `6.14 × 9.21` · `7 × 10` · `8.25 × 11`

Note 8.25 × 11 (hardcover) is a different trim from 8.5 × 11 (paperback).

## 4. Interior margins and gutter

The inside (gutter) margin is a **minimum that grows with page count**, because
a thicker book curls further into its own binding.

| Page count | Inside (gutter) | Outside/top/bottom, no bleed | Outside/top/bottom, with bleed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 24–150 | 0.375 in (9.6 mm) | 0.25 in (6.4 mm) | 0.375 in (9.6 mm) |
| 151–300 | 0.5 in (12.7 mm) | 0.25 in | 0.375 in |
| 301–500 | 0.625 in (15.9 mm) | 0.25 in | 0.375 in |
| 501–700 | 0.75 in (19.1 mm) | 0.25 in | 0.375 in |
| 701–828 | 0.875 in (22.3 mm) | 0.25 in | 0.375 in |

Above 828 pages the table ends — that combination does not exist, rather than
defaulting to the nearest band.

- Top, bottom and outside do **not** have to match each other; each just has to
  clear its minimum.
- The gutter is the **inside** margin. If your layout tool has separate "inside
  margin" and "gutter" fields (Word does), inside + gutter must reach the number.
- Mirror the layout: gutter left on odd/recto pages, right on even/verso.
- Bleed raises the outside/top/bottom minimum from 0.25 in to 0.375 in. It does
  **not** change the inside minimum.

**The circular trap.** Widening the gutter narrows the text block, which makes
the book longer, which can push it over a band threshold into a wider gutter
requirement. A manuscript at 298 pages with a 0.5 in gutter is not safely inside
the 151–300 band — set the gutter, re-flow, and it may land at 304, where KDP
wants 0.625 in. Always re-check the page count after setting the gutter.

## 5. Bleed

Bleed is 0.125 in (3.2 mm). It applies to the **top, bottom and outside** edges
only — never the binding edge, because that edge is not cut.

| Trim | Interior page size with bleed |
|---|---|
| 5 × 8 | 5.125 × 8.25 in |
| 5.5 × 8.5 | 5.625 × 8.75 in |
| 6 × 9 | 6.125 × 9.25 in |
| 7 × 10 | 7.125 × 10.25 in |
| 8.5 × 11 | 8.625 × 11.25 in |

Bleed is only supported in fixed-format files. Most text-only interiors do not
need it — use it when images or background colour must run to the edge.

## 6. Spine width

```
spine = page count × thickness per page
```

| Paper | Thickness per page |
|---|---|
| B&W white | 0.002252 in (0.0572 mm) |
| B&W cream | 0.0025 in (0.0635 mm) |
| Standard colour, white | 0.002252 in (0.0572 mm) |
| Premium colour, white | 0.002347 in (0.0596 mm) |

Spine text is printed only on books of **more than 79 pages** (Cover Creator
says 80). Keep text at least **0.0625 in (1.6 mm)** clear of both fold lines,
and allow ±0.0625 in variance on either side of each fold.

## 7. Page count rules

- Minimum 24 pages for a paperback (72 on standard colour), 75 for hardcover.
- KDP computes the count from your file and **rounds an odd count up to even**.
  An odd page count is not a rejection — it just gains a blank final page.
- Page numbering runs sequentially, even on the left and odd on the right for
  left-to-right books. Roman numerals are allowed for the front matter.
- A series may continue numbering from a previous volume if the series name and
  volume number are labelled.

## 8. Hardcover specifics

A hardcover is a **case-laminate** binding: printed paper wrapped over board.
There is no dust jacket.

- Trim: one of five (above), 75–550 pages.
- Standard colour is not offered; use premium colour or black and white.
- The cover file includes **wrap** (the part glued to the inside of the case) and
  **hinges** (where the case bends when opened), so it is much larger than a
  paperback cover for the same trim.
- KDP does not publish a spine formula for hardcover. See
  `cover-geometry.md` for the geometry and how to confirm it.
- A headband (black-and-white) is added at the top and bottom of the spine when
  the manuscript exceeds 120 pages.
